## Strategy 1: The "We Miss You" Win-Back Campaign (For Inactive Contacts)

Every business has customers who once bought but haven’t returned in months. These contacts are **far easier to re-engage** than cold leads because they already know your brand, trust your product, and have a history with you.

### How to Run This Campaign
1. **Segment your audience**
   - Use WhatsApp Business tools or CRM integrations to filter contacts by last purchase date (e.g., inactive for 90+ days).
   - Example: Restaurants can target customers who haven’t ordered in 3+ months.

2. **Craft a message that reignites curiosity**
   - Avoid generic "We miss you!" lines. Instead, **offer immediate value** to grab attention.
   - **Template:**
     > *Hi [Name], we noticed you haven’t tried our [product/service] lately! As a thank-you, here’s **20% off your next order**—valid for 72 hours only. Use code **WELCOMEBACK20**. Let us know if you’d like recommendations! 😊
     > *P.S. [Brief reason to act now, e.g., "Limited stock on [popular item]!" or "Our chef’s special is back for a limited time!"]"

3. **Follow up smartly**
   - If no response after 48 hours, send a **polite follow-up**:
     > *Hi [Name], just checking in—did you see our 20% offer? We’d love to have you back! 🛒"
   - If still no reply, **ditch the discount** and try a **social proof message**:
     > *P.S. Over 500 customers just tried [product] this week—here’s what they’re saying: [short testimonial] 👇"

### Pro Tips
- **Timing matters:** Send during **high-engagement hours** (e.g., 11 AM–2 PM or 7 PM–9 PM on weekdays).
- **Personalize:** Use merge tags (e.g., `[Name]`) to make messages feel 1:1.
- **Track results:** If open rates are below 30%, tweak your subject line or offer.
