## Tactic #1: Instant Welcome Messages That Don’t Feel Robotic

Your Instagram bio says a lot—but what happens when someone sends you a DM? Most brands either:
- Ignore it completely (losing the lead), or
- Send a generic "Thanks for messaging!" reply (missing the chance to engage).

**Automation fixes this.**

Use a **smart welcome message** that:
- Acknowledges the follower by name
- Offers value upfront (e.g., a discount, free guide, or exclusive tip)
- Guides them toward the next step (e.g., "Reply with ‘MENU’ to see our bestsellers")

**Example:**
```
Hi [First Name]! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to [Your Brand].
We’d love to help you—here’s a **10% discount** on your first order: [LINK]
Just reply with ‘ORDER’ to claim it!
```

This approach is **personal, action-driven, and conversion-focused**—no robot vibes.
