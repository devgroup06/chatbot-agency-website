WhatsApp is where your customers already are. Over two billion people use it daily, and for many businesses it has quietly become the number one channel for questions, orders and bookings. The problem? Messages arrive at midnight, during rush hour, on weekends — and every unanswered message is a customer walking to a competitor.

A WhatsApp chatbot fixes that. Here's everything a small business owner needs to know.

## What a WhatsApp Chatbot Actually Does

A WhatsApp chatbot is an AI assistant connected to your business's WhatsApp number through the official WhatsApp Cloud API. When a customer messages you, the bot:

- Answers questions instantly using your business data (menu, services, prices, timings, policies)
- Takes orders and booking requests, and confirms them automatically
- Captures the customer's name and details as a lead
- Hands the conversation to a human when needed

Unlike auto-replies, a modern AI chatbot understands natural language. "Do you have anything spicy that's vegetarian?" gets a real answer, not a menu link.

## Why Small Businesses See the Biggest Wins

Large companies have call centers. Small businesses have an owner with a phone that never stops buzzing. That's exactly why automation pays off fastest for small teams:

1. **Zero missed messages.** The bot replies in under a second, at 3 PM or 3 AM.
2. **More completed sales.** Fast answers keep buying intent alive — studies consistently show response time is the biggest driver of conversion in chat.
3. **A growing customer list.** Every conversation becomes a contact you can re-engage with broadcasts and reminders.
4. **Your time back.** The bot handles the 80% of questions that repeat every day; you handle the 20% that matter.

## Real Use Cases by Industry

- **Restaurants** — menu questions, table bookings, order taking, delivery status
- **Salons & clinics** — appointment booking, service prices, reminders that cut no-shows
- **E-commerce** — product availability, sizes, order tracking, returns
- **Schools & institutes** — admissions questions, fee structures, class timings
- **Real estate** — listing details, viewing appointments, lead qualification

## How to Launch in 5 Steps

1. **Get on the official API.** Automation requires the WhatsApp Cloud API (not the regular app). An agency partner sets this up for you.
2. **Feed the bot your business data.** Menus, price lists, FAQs, policies — this becomes the bot's knowledge.
3. **Design the key flows.** Ordering, booking, lead capture — whatever drives your revenue.
4. **Test with real questions.** Ask the bot everything your customers ask you.
5. **Go live and monitor.** Watch conversations, ratings and leads in your dashboard, and keep improving.

## What About Broadcasts and Reminders?

The official API also unlocks **template broadcasts** — scheduled messages to opted-in customers for offers and announcements — and **automated reminders** for appointments and payments. For most businesses these features alone pay for the bot.
