## Common Mistakes Small Businesses Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Chat automation isn’t magic—it requires the right strategy. Here’s what to watch out for:

### ❌ **Over-Automating (Losing the Human Touch)**
**Mistake:** Setting up a bot that answers everything—even when a human is needed.
**Fix:** Use **hybrid support**—let the bot handle simple queries but escalate complex issues to your team.

### ❌ **Ignoring Follow-Ups (Leaving Money on the Table)**
**Mistake:** Autoresponding once and never engaging again.
**Fix:** Set up **drip campaigns** to nurture leads over time (e.g., send a reminder if they don’t open your first message).

### ❌ **Not Testing Your Chatbot (Broken Flows = Lost Sales)**
**Mistake:** Assuming your bot works because it’s live.
**Fix:** Test every possible customer path (e.g., "What happens if someone asks about a refund?").

### ❌ **Choosing a Platform That Doesn’t Fit Your Audience**
**Mistake:** Using Instagram DMs for a B2B SaaS business.
**Fix:** Pick the channel your customers **actually use** (e.g., WhatsApp for local services, Messenger for e-commerce).
