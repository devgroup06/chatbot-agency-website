It's 11:47 PM. Someone is scrolling your Instagram, craving biryani, and they DM you: *"Are you open tomorrow? Do you deliver to Gulberg?"* Nobody answers until morning. By then, they've ordered from someone else.

This exact scene plays out for every restaurant, every night. Here's how AI chatbots are ending it.

## The Restaurant Chat Problem

Restaurants get the same questions on repeat, across four different inboxes:

- "What's on the menu?" / "Do you have vegetarian options?"
- "How much is the family deal?"
- "Are you open right now?"
- "Do you deliver to my area?"
- "Can I book a table for 6 on Friday?"

Each question is a sale waiting to happen — but staff are busy cooking and serving, not typing. The result: slow replies, missed orders, empty tables that could have been booked.

## What a Restaurant Chatbot Handles

A restaurant-trained AI bot connects to your WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger and website widget, and handles the full front-of-house conversation:

### Menu & Pricing Questions
The bot knows your full menu — items, prices, portions, spice levels, dietary tags. Natural questions get natural answers: "What's good for two people under 2000 rupees?" actually works.

### Order Taking
Customers build an order in chat; the bot confirms items, totals and delivery details, then notifies your team instantly. No app download, no account creation — just the chat they already opened.

### Table Bookings
Party size, date, time — captured and confirmed in seconds, with automatic reminders that cut no-shows.

### Delivery Questions
Areas, minimums, timings and charges — answered instantly, so the order doesn't die on a logistics question.

## The Off-Hours Goldmine

Here's what surprises most restaurant owners: **a huge share of chat inquiries arrive when the kitchen is closed** — late night cravings, next-day planning, weekend event bookings. A bot doesn't take those orders for right now; it captures them for *later*:

> "We open at 12 PM tomorrow! Want me to save your order for our first delivery slot? 🍛"

Midnight craving → morning order. That's revenue that simply didn't exist before.

## Broadcasts: The Repeat-Order Engine

Every chat customer becomes a contact. With WhatsApp template broadcasts, Tuesday's slow night becomes:

> "Chef's special tonight: 20% off all handi dishes. Reply ORDER to grab yours 🔥"

Sent to hundreds of past customers in one click, with delivery tracking. Restaurants consistently find this their highest-ROI marketing channel — the audience already knows and likes the food.
